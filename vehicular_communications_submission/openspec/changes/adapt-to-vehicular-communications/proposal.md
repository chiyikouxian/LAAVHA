## Why

The manuscript was originally prepared for MDPI Remote Sensing but needs to be adapted for submission to Elsevier's Vehicular Communications journal. The journal focuses on vehicle networks, connected vehicles, and V2X communication systems, requiring a reframing of our UAV vertical handover work from a "remote sensing" perspective to a "vehicular communications" perspective while preserving the core technical contributions.

## What Changes

- Reframe abstract and introduction to emphasize vehicular communications and V2X applications instead of remote sensing
- Update related work section to include more vehicular communications and V2X literature
- Adjust problem motivation to highlight UAV integration in vehicular networks (e.g., traffic monitoring, roadside assistance, V2X relay)
- Ensure application scenarios align with journal scope (UAVs as mobile network nodes in vehicular contexts)
- Verify reference formatting matches Elsevier/Vehicular Communications requirements
- Review figures and captions to ensure they align with vehicular communications terminology
- Core algorithm (LAAVHA/ALERA) and experimental results remain unchanged
