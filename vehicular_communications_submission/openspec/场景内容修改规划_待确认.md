### 📍 位置1：摘要第1-2句（第82行）

**当前内容**：
```latex
Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) remote-sensing systems support large-area Earth observation, including disaster assessment and environmental monitoring. During cruise missions, UAVs frequently cross fifth-generation (5G), Long-Term Evolution (LTE), and Wi-Fi coverage boundaries, creating vertical-handover (VHO) challenges...
```

**修改为**：
```latex
Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) are increasingly deployed in intelligent transportation systems (ITS) and vehicular communications, serving as temporary network infrastructure for traffic monitoring, vehicle-to-everything (V2X) relay, and roadside assistance. During aerial cruise, UAVs frequently traverse heterogeneous wireless network boundaries—fifth-generation (5G), Long-Term Evolution (LTE), and Wi-Fi—creating vertical-handover (VHO) challenges involving decision latency, ping-pong handovers, and fixed attribute weights that cannot adapt to changing flight phases.
```

**改动点**：
- 删除"remote-sensing systems"和"Earth observation"

- 定位UAV为"temporary network infrastructure"
- 保留异构网络切换挑战（技术核心不变）

**修改为**：


- **开篇直接强调**：ITS、V2X、移动性管理挑战
