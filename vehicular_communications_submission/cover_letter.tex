We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled \textbf{``Risk-Aware Predictive Vertical Handover for UAV Communications in Heterogeneous Wireless Networks''} for consideration for publication in \textit{Vehicular Communications}.

\textbf{Summary of the work.}
Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) operating in heterogeneous wireless networks face significant vertical handover (VHO) challenges, including decision latency, ping-pong handovers, and fixed attribute weights that cannot adapt to dynamic channel conditions. This paper proposes LAAVHA, an adaptive VHO algorithm that integrates stacked-LSTM state prediction, attention-based dynamic weighting, improved TOPSIS with fused current and predicted states, and dual-hysteresis decision-making. LAAVHA is further enhanced with adaptive dynamic hysteresis (ADH) and risk-sensitive TOPSIS (RS-TOPSIS) to form ALERA. Across 250 extended 50-second trajectories, LAAVHA achieves the lowest mean handover count among 11 algorithms, reducing handovers by 46\%--95\% versus eight baselines while maintaining the highest throughput and lowest delay. ALERA preserves necessary handovers under sustained degradation while suppressing false handovers under transient channel variation.

\textbf{Relevance to Vehicular Communications.}
This work addresses handover decision-making for aerial vehicles in heterogeneous wireless networks, a topic of growing importance as UAVs are increasingly integrated into 5G and beyond-5G infrastructure. The proposed algorithms are directly applicable to vehicle-to-network communication scenarios, making this work well suited for the scope of \textit{Vehicular Communications}.
